A watt-hour means nothing to most people. The same answer counted in phone charges or cups of coffee does. At the Digital Society School I helped build decision tools for a Dutch government partner around exactly that translation — because the gap between the most and least efficient model on the same task can exceed an order of magnitude, and few tools put that in front of the person typing the prompt.

The point is not AI guilt. It's the same soft-path thinking I learned in water management: before you build a bigger pipe — or call a bigger model — ask whether the demand itself can be smarter. Try the [EcoPrompt Coach chart](index.html#ecoprompt) on the homepage; that's the argument, running live.
