My Wageningen thesis ended with "Dirty Delta" — a deliberately dark storyline for Africa's coastal waters by mid-century. Model output alone seldom moves a decision; a story people can picture often does. Turning regression coefficients on income, urbanisation and agriculture into a narrative was the most useful translation work I've done.

The lesson survived the thesis: if your analysis can't be retold in a corridor after the meeting, it rarely counts. I now write the storyline first and let it set the questions for the numbers, never the answers.
